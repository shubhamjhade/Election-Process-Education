Quick Election Steps

  1. 1

    Voter Registration

    Register online via the NVSP portal or fill Form 6 at your local ERO office. You must be 18+ on the qualifying date.

  2. 2

    Check Electoral Roll

    Verify your name on the electoral roll at electoralsearch.eci.gov.in before polling day.

  3. 3

    Polling Day

    Visit your designated booth with a valid Photo ID (EPIC). Cast your vote using the EVM and verify via VVPAT.

  4. 4

    Counting & Results

    Votes are counted under ECI supervision. Results are declared and the winning candidate is announced.
